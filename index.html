The collection

Resin dishes, coasters and wall pieces, and 3D-printed vases, lamps and desk objects. Tap any piece for details.

Two materials, both made with patience.

Epoxy resin

Each piece is poured by hand, in small batches, and left to cure for days. Pigments are mixed by eye, so no two pieces come out exactly alike.

3D printing

Objects are designed on screen and printed layer by layer, then cleaned and finished by hand. The fine layer lines are part of the look.
